# Code of Conduct
 
## Our Pledge
 
We are committed to making Aulthium a welcoming, respectful, and constructive open-source project for everyone.
 
We expect contributors, maintainers, users, and community members to participate in good faith and treat others with respect.
 
## Expected Behavior
 
Examples of positive behavior include:
 
 
- Being respectful and constructive.
 
- Welcoming people with different experience levels.
 
- Giving useful technical feedback.
 
- Focusing criticism on code, ideas, and proposals rather than people.
 
- Being patient with beginners.
 
- Respecting different opinions and approaches.
 
- Keeping discussions relevant and productive.
 
- Respecting people's privacy and boundaries.
 

 
## Unacceptable Behavior
 
The following behavior is not acceptable:
 
 
- Harassment or intimidation.
 
- Personal attacks.
 
- Threats or targeted abuse.
 
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
 
- Doxxing or sharing private information without permission.
 
- Deliberate disruption of project infrastructure.
 
- Malicious behavior toward contributors or users.
 
- Sexual harassment or inappropriate conduct.
 
- Trolling intended to prevent productive discussion.
 

 
## Technical Disagreements
 
Technical disagreements are normal in open-source development.
 
When disagreements happen:
 
 
1. Explain your reasoning.
 
2. Provide evidence or examples when possible.
 
3. Discuss the technical issue rather than attacking the person.
 
4. Accept that maintainers may make a different decision.
 
5. Keep discussions constructive.
 

 
Being passionate about Aulthium is encouraged. Being hostile toward other contributors is not.
 
## Maintainer Responsibilities
 
Maintainers are responsible for helping keep project spaces constructive.
 
Depending on the situation, maintainers may:
 
 
- Ask participants to change their behavior.
 
- Remove inappropriate content.
 
- Close discussions or issues.
 
- Reject or request changes to contributions.
 
- Temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
 

 
Actions will be taken based on the circumstances and severity of the behavior.
 
## Scope
 
This Code of Conduct applies to spaces used for Aulthium development and community interaction, including:
 
 
- GitHub issues
 
- Pull requests
 
- Discussions
 
- Code reviews
 
- Project-related community spaces
 
- Other spaces where someone is representing the Aulthium project
 

 
## Reporting Problems
 
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the Aulthium maintainers through an appropriate GitHub channel.
 
Please include enough information for the maintainers to understand what happened.
 
Do not publicly post sensitive personal information or private evidence.
 
## Good-Faith Participation
 
Mistakes happen.
 
Not every disagreement or poorly worded message is misconduct. The goal of this Code of Conduct is not to punish honest mistakes, but to maintain a community where people can learn, contribute, and collaborate.
 
## Final Note
 
Aulthium is built by its community.
 
Be curious. Be constructive. Help others learn.
 
**Build better software together. 🚀**
